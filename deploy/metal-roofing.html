Metal Roofing in Alabama, Explained Straight

We install both metal and architectural shingle roofs, so we have no dog in the fight. Here is the honest comparison, and how to tell which one your home and your plans actually call for.

Both Systems, One Standard

The Roofer Who Sells Both
Can Tell You the Truth

A shingle-only company will talk you out of metal. A metal-only company will talk you into it. We install both systems to manufacturer spec, which means the recommendation you get from us is based on your roof, your budget, and how long you plan to stay, not on what we happen to sell.

Sometimes that answer is metal. Often, honestly, it is a quality architectural shingle. Either way you will see the real numbers for both, side by side, from one licensed Alabama contractor instead of two competing sales pitches.

The Honest Comparison

Metal vs. Architectural Shingles

Lifespan. Metal is a 40 to 70 year roof. Architectural shingles give you 20 to 30 in Alabama conditions. If you are putting a roof on the house you plan to grow old in, that difference is the whole conversation.

Upfront cost. Metal typically runs two to three times the price of shingles installed. Over the life of the home it can come out cheaper because you buy one roof instead of two, but the bigger check still has to clear this year, and financing only softens that so far. We will never pretend otherwise.

Alabama weather. Both systems, installed right, handle our heat, humidity, and storm seasons. Hail dents metal cosmetically but rarely breaks its seal, while hail can bruise shingles in ways that shorten their life and open the door to an insurance claim. High wind favors standing seam metal, though a properly nailed architectural shingle is no slouch.

Heat and energy. Metal reflects summer sun instead of soaking it up, which means cooler attics and often a little off the power bill. Shingle technology has improved here too, and attic ventilation matters as much as the surface. We check ventilation on every job either way.

Looks and resale. A crisp standing seam roof turns heads and suits farmhouse, coastal, and modern styles. Architectural shingles remain what most Alabama buyers expect to see, and a fresh shingle roof is one of the cleanest signals a house is well kept. Neither choice hurts resale when the work is done right.

The Straight Answer

Which One Should You Choose?

Metal makes sense when you plan to stay 20 or more years, when you want the last roof you will ever buy, when energy performance matters to you, or when your home's style calls for it. It is a premium product and it earns its price over decades, not overnight.

Shingles win when the budget is real, when you may sell within ten years, or when the neighborhood look matters. A CertainTeed architectural shingle roof installed to spec is a genuinely excellent roof, and it is what we put on most Alabama homes, including for homeowners who asked us about metal first and appreciated hearing the honest math.

Not sure? That is what the free consultation is for. We measure your actual roof, price both systems on it, and walk you through the numbers with no pressure in either direction. Whichever way you go, the same installation standard applies: decking inspected, flashing done right, property protected, and a final walkthrough before we call it finished.

FAQ

Metal Roofing Questions, Answered

Is a metal roof loud when it rains?

Not the way people picture. That tin-roof drumming comes from metal over open framing, like a barn or a porch. A residential metal roof goes over solid decking and underlayment, which absorbs the sound. Inside the house, rain on metal is about as loud as rain on shingles. Most homeowners cannot tell the difference.

Can you put a metal roof on an older home?

Usually, yes. Metal is actually lighter than most shingle systems, so weight is rarely the issue. What matters is the condition of the decking and the framing underneath, which we inspect before quoting anything. On a house with character, the right metal profile can suit the home beautifully, and we will tell you honestly if we think it will not.

Does a metal roof lower my home insurance in Alabama?

It can help, and it is worth a call to your agent before you decide. Some Alabama insurers offer credits for impact-resistant roofing, and metal holds up well in hail and high wind. We will not promise you a specific discount, because your carrier sets that, but we will give you the product documentation your agent needs to check.

What is the difference between standing seam and exposed fastener metal?

Standing seam hides the fasteners under raised ribs, so there are no screw heads exposed to the weather. It costs more and lasts longer with less maintenance. Exposed fastener panels are screwed straight through the metal, cost less up front, and need the fasteners checked and eventually resealed over the years. We install both and will walk you through which fits your roof and your budget.

How long does a metal roof last compared to shingles in Alabama?

A quality metal roof is a 40 to 70 year system. Architectural shingles give you 20 to 30 years here. Metal costs more up front, often two to three times as much, so the honest question is how long you plan to own the home. Stay 20 or more years and metal often wins the math. Sell in five and shingles usually make more sense.

Do metal roofs get hotter in the Alabama sun?

The opposite, usually. Metal reflects more of the sun's heat than dark shingles absorb, and lighter colors amplify the effect. Many homeowners see cooler attics and a little relief on summer cooling bills. Color choice matters, and we will show you the options with the reflectivity numbers rather than guesses.
