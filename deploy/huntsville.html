Roofing Done Right

Huntsville is growing fast, and roofs here range from mid-century homes in Five Points and Blossomwood to brand-new builds across Madison and Harvest. Each one needs a different eye, and we bring one that is not trying to sell every homeowner the same job.

North Alabama gets its share of hail and high straight-line winds through the spring severe-weather season. Wind damage is especially easy to miss: creased shingles look fine from the ground but have already lost their seal, and the leak shows up months later. Our inspection catches it and documents it properly.

How much does a new roof cost in Huntsville?

It depends on the size, pitch, and complexity of your roof and the materials you choose. We provide a free inspection and an itemized, transparent quote with no hidden fees. If your roof was damaged by a storm, your policy may cover most of the replacement cost.

What roofing materials do you use?

We use CertainTeed products and are a CertainTeed certified contractor. We install premium architectural shingles and other manufacturer-spec systems, chosen to stand up to Alabama heat, humidity, and severe storms for decades.

Can you help with a storm damage insurance claim?

Yes. We document all damage with clear photos, meet your adjuster on site, and guide your claim step by step so the process stays smooth and nothing legitimate gets missed. You stay in control of the claim the whole way.
