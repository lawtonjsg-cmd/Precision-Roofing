FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are completed in a single day. Larger or more complex roofs may take two, and we'll give you a clear, realistic timeline before we ever start.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We inspect your roof, document the damage with clear photos, and work directly with your adjuster to make the claim process as smooth and stress-free as possible.

What roofing materials do you use?

We use CertainTeed products and are a CertainTeed certified contractor. We install premium architectural shingles and other manufacturer-spec systems, chosen to stand up to Alabama heat, humidity, and severe storms for decades.

Do you offer free estimates?

Absolutely. Every roof inspection and estimate is completely free and no-obligation, just honest findings and a transparent, itemized quote.

Do you offer financing options?

Yes. Flexible financing is available so a new roof can fit your budget. Ask us about current plans and terms during your free estimate.
