About Precision Roofing Alabama

Alabama's trusted, licensed & insured roofing contractor for replacements, repairs, and storm-damage insurance claims. Built on honesty, quality, and a commitment to protecting your home.

Who Are We?

Alabama's Trusted
Roofing Contractor

At Precision Roofing Alabama, we're a locally owned and operated roofing company proudly serving homeowners across the entire State of Alabama. We specialize in roof replacements, repairs, and storm-damage restoration — delivering dependable solutions that protect, restore, and strengthen homes of every size.

Whether it's a full architectural-shingle replacement, a stubborn leak, or hail and severe-storm damage, our crews are committed to quality craftsmanship and professional execution. We use premium, manufacturer-spec materials, proven installation methods, and maintain clear communication from start to finish.

For homeowners, we focus on protecting your investment and your peace of mind — handling insurance claims directly with your adjuster so the process stays smooth, fair, and stress-free from inspection to final nail.

At Precision Roofing Alabama, integrity, attention to detail, and doing the job right the first time aren't just promises — they're our standard.
