Pricing

What Does a New Roof Cost in Alabama?

Every roof is different, but the price comes down to four things: size and pitch of the roof, the shingle line you choose, how many layers have to come off, and the condition of the decking underneath. We give you an itemized written estimate up front. The number we quote is the number you pay unless we find hidden damage, and if we do, we photograph it and talk it through with you before touching a thing.

Two ways to make it easier: if your roof failed from storm or hail damage, your insurance may owe you a roof, and we handle that claim process for you. And for every roof, flexible financing can turn a big year into a manageable monthly payment.

FAQ

Roof Replacement Questions, Answered

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most Alabama homes are completed in a single day. Larger or more complex roofs may take two, and we give you a clear, realistic timeline before we ever start.

What affects the cost of a roof replacement in Alabama?

Roof size and pitch, the shingle line you choose, how many layers need tearing off, and whether any decking needs replacing once we open it up. Every estimate is free, written, and itemized, so there are no surprises on build day.

Do you offer financing for a new roof?

Yes. Flexible financing options are available so a needed roof doesn't have to wait on a lump sum. Ask during your free inspection and we'll walk you through the choices.

Will insurance pay for my roof replacement?

If the roof failed from sudden storm or hail damage, your homeowners policy likely covers it, and we handle the entire claim process for you. If the roof is simply worn out from age, that's typically not covered, and we'll give you honest numbers and financing options instead.
