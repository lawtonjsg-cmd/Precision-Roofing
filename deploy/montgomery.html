Montgomery Roofing Questions

Do you serve Montgomery, Alabama?

Yes. We provide roof replacements, roof repairs, and storm-damage insurance claim help throughout Montgomery and the surrounding area, including Montgomery, Prattville, Wetumpka, Millbrook, Pike Road. Call (334) 303-6398 for a free inspection.

How much does a new roof cost in Montgomery?

It depends on the size, pitch, and complexity of your roof and the materials you choose. We provide a free inspection and an itemized, transparent quote with no hidden fees. If your roof was damaged by a storm, your policy may cover most of the replacement cost.

What roofing materials do you use?

We use CertainTeed products and are a CertainTeed certified contractor. We install premium architectural shingles and other manufacturer-spec systems, chosen to stand up to Alabama heat, humidity, and severe storms for decades.

Can you help with a storm damage insurance claim?

Yes. We document all damage with clear photos, meet your adjuster on site, and work the claim directly with your insurance company so the process stays smooth and you get the coverage you're owed.

How fast can you get out to my home?

In most cases we can schedule your free inspection within a few days, and sooner after a major storm event. Call (334) 303-6398 and we'll get you on the schedule.
