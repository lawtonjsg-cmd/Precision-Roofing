FAQ

Before You Call, A Few Common Questions

How fast will someone get back to me?

Within one business day for form submissions and emails, and usually the same day. Active leaks and storm damage go to the front of the line, so call (334) 318-7255 directly if water is getting into your home right now.

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes, completely free and with no obligation. We inspect the roof, photograph what we find, and hand you a written estimate. There is no charge whether you hire us or not, and no one is going to call you six times afterward.

What information should I have ready?

Your address, roughly how old the roof is if you know, what you are seeing inside or outside the house, and whether a recent storm came through. If you have already filed an insurance claim, have your claim number handy. None of this is required, it just helps us help you faster.

Do you charge for an estimate after a storm?

No. Post-storm inspections are free, including the photo documentation you would need for a claim. We will tell you honestly whether you have enough damage to be worth filing on, because a claim that gets denied helps nobody.

Are you licensed and insured in Alabama?

We are. Precision Roofing Alabama LLC carries Alabama Home Builder license HB # 41012 and full liability and workers compensation coverage, and we are a CertainTeed certified contractor. We are glad to provide proof of insurance before any work begins.
