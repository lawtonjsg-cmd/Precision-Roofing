Our Service Area

Precision Roofing Alabama serves homeowners across the entire State of Alabama, with crews working daily in these five major markets.

Montgomery, AL

Our home base. From Cloverdale's older homes to the new builds in Pike Road, we know the River Region's roofs, its heat, and its spring hail lines.

See Montgomery Services

Birmingham, AL

Jefferson and Shelby County sit in the heart of Dixie Alley. Hail and straight-line wind damage here is rarely visible from the driveway, so we document every finding with photos.

See Birmingham Services

Huntsville, AL

Mid-century homes in Five Points and brand-new builds across Madison and Harvest need different eyes. Wind-creased shingles are the damage we catch most in North Alabama.

See Huntsville Services

Auburn & Opelika, AL

Long humid summers drive algae streaking and decking moisture across Lee County. We inspect the full system, and we work well with rental and investment property owners.

See Auburn & Opelika Services

Baldwin County, AL

Gulf Coast roofing is its own discipline. Hurricane wind uplift and salt air find every shortcut, so we install high-wind CertainTeed systems built for the coast.

See Baldwin County Services

FAQ

Service Area Questions, Answered

What areas of Alabama does Precision Roofing Alabama serve?

We serve homeowners across the State of Alabama, with crews working regularly in five areas: the Montgomery region, the Birmingham metro, Huntsville and North Alabama, Auburn, Opelika and Lee County, and Baldwin County on the Gulf Coast. That covers roughly 60 towns and communities listed on this page.

Do you charge a travel fee for outlying areas?

No. The roof inspection and the written estimate are free anywhere we serve, and we do not add a trip charge for driving out to you. If you are far enough outside our normal range that we cannot get to you properly, we will say so honestly on the phone rather than waste your time.

My town is not on the list. Can you still help?

Most likely, yes. The communities on this page are the ones we work in most often, not a hard boundary. Give us a call at (334) 318-7255 and we will tell you straight away whether we cover your address. We cover most of Alabama.

How quickly can you get to my area after a storm?

Storm damage and active leaks go to the front of the line everywhere we work. After a major hail or wind event we prioritize the hardest hit areas first, and in most of our service area we can be out to look within a day or two. See our storm and hail damage page for what to do in the meantime.

Are you licensed to work throughout Alabama?

Yes. Precision Roofing Alabama LLC holds Alabama Home Builder license HB # 41012, carries full liability and workers compensation coverage, and is a CertainTeed certified contractor. That license is statewide, so the same credentials apply whether we are working in Huntsville or Gulf Shores.

Do you handle insurance claims in every area you serve?

We do. Wherever we work, we document the damage, meet your adjuster on site, and handle the claim process directly with your carrier. Alabama sees hail inland and wind and water on the coast, so claim work is a large part of what we do in every one of these regions.

Don't See Your Area?

We cover most of Alabama. Give us a call to confirm your area. We likely serve it.
